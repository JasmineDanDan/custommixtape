About Custom Mixtape

Small musical gifts with a cassette feeling.

Custom Mixtape turns a few songs, a short note and a favorite visual style into a personal digital gift that is easy to share.

What Custom Mixtape is

Custom Mixtape is a browser-based mixtape maker for creating a small musical moment for someone special. It brings back the feeling of a handmade cassette without requiring a physical tape, a new account or complicated setup.

How it works

  • Choose a cassette backdrop and add a few keepsake stickers.
  • Select songs from the available music previews and arrange their order.
  • Write a short note, finish the card and share the gift link.

Made for real moments

A Custom Mixtape gift can mark a birthday, anniversary, thank-you, friendship, new beginning or an ordinary day that deserves a little more care. The recipient can open the card in a browser and start the music when they are ready.

Make a matching paper insert

For a more physical keepsake, use the Custom J Cards tool to create a printable cassette J card with an album title, dedication and track list.
