Create a Mixtape for Someone You Love

Choose a cassette look, add up to four songs, write a little note, and share a playable music gift with one link.

A music gift they can open

Each finished card includes a personal note, a cassette-style design and selected songs that the recipient can open and play in a browser without an account.

A Small Music Gift, Made Personal

  1. Pick songs with a reason. Choose up to four openly licensed songs and place them in the order you want someone to hear them.
  2. Make the card feel like them. Choose a cassette backdrop and up to three little stickers for a birthday, friendship, anniversary or quiet everyday moment.
  3. Give them a moment to open. Add a note, create a link and send a gift they can open in their browser without creating an account.

Start With the Moment

Make a birthday surprise, a gift for a best friend, a love or anniversary card, a little comfort, or something just because.

How It Works

  1. Customize your cassette. Choose a cassette design and a few stickers that fit the person, the music or the occasion.
  2. Add songs and a personal note. Preview songs, arrange them in order and write a short message.
  3. Share your digital gift. Finish the card and send one memorable gift link.

A Few Useful Things to Know

Does the recipient need an account?

No. Anyone with the gift link can open your mixtape for you in a browser.

Can the recipient play the songs?

Yes. The shared gift keeps the selected openly licensed songs and their order.

Where does my mixtape live before I share it?

Your mixtape stays in this browser while you make it. The gift link carries the card settings when you are ready to share.

Can I make a matching cassette J card?

Yes. Use Custom J cards to create a printable cassette insert with an album title, dedication, and track list.

How many songs can I add?

You can choose up to four songs and arrange them in the order you want them played.

Is the mixtape maker free?

Yes. You can create and share a mixtape for free.
