Free Mixtape Maker

Create a personalized digital mixtape with a retro cassette design, stickers, songs, and a personal note. Finish it as a shareable music gift.

Make your mixtape in four steps

  1. Choose a cassette look. Pick Meadow Sprig, Lemon Windowpane, Coral Ribbon or Powder Constellation.
  2. Add up to three stickers. Choose details such as a birthday cake, gift box, tulip, coffee, rainbow or rose.
  3. Pick and arrange up to four songs. Preview the site's openly licensed songs, filter them by scene and set their order.
  4. Write a note and share your gift. Add To, From and a personal note, then create one share link.

What the mixtape maker supports

To and From names allow up to 40 characters each. The personal note allows up to 140 characters. A recipient can open the finished mixtape in a browser without an account and press play to hear the selected songs.

Start with a moment

Choose Birthday, For a friend, Love & anniversary or A little comfort, then select songs that fit the feeling.

Mixtape maker FAQ

How many songs can I add?

You can choose up to four songs and arrange them in the order you want them played.

Can I preview the songs?

Yes. Each available song has a preview control.

Does the recipient need an account?

No. Anyone with the share link can open the finished mixtape in a browser.

Where is my unfinished mixtape saved?

Your unfinished work is saved in this browser while you make it.
