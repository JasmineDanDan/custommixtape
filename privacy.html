Privacy & data

Your gift stays yours to share.

Custom Mixtape is designed to work without an account. This page explains how the current website handles gift details, shared links and music playback.

Where your work lives

While you are making a gift, its details stay in the browser you are using. Custom Mixtape does not ask you to create an account for the current version of the service.

What a share link contains

When you copy a gift link, the selected look, songs, title, recipient name, sender name and note are included in the link so the recipient can open the same gift. Anyone with that link can view those details.

Do not add passwords, addresses, phone numbers, financial information or any other sensitive information to a gift note.

Music playback

Music previews are loaded from the listed music source only after someone presses play. See Music credits for the current track sources and licences.

Third-party services

Music previews are loaded from the sources listed on the Music credits page. Our hosting and security providers may process standard technical information, such as an IP address, browser type and request time, to deliver and protect the website. Custom Mixtape does not require an account or ask for payment details.

Advertising and cookies

Custom Mixtape may display ads provided by Google. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this and other websites.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps and manage personalized advertising choices in My Ad Center.

Choices for visitors

Where required, including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Custom Mixtape will request the required consent before personalized advertising is shown. Visitors can change their advertising choices through the consent controls provided on the site when available.

Changes to this page

We may update this policy when the service, its data practices or the advertising services we use change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
