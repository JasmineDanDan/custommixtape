FAQ

Questions about your digital mixtape gift.

Here are the practical details about making, sharing and using a Tape Note card.

Does the recipient need an account?

No. Anyone with the gift link can open the mixtape in a browser. The recipient does not need to create an account.

How do I make a mixtape?

Choose a cassette look, add stickers, select one to four songs, write a note and finish the gift. The finished card gives you a share link to send.

Can the recipient play the songs?

Yes. The shared gift keeps the selected openly licensed songs and their order. Playback begins only after the recipient chooses to press play.

Where does my draft live?

While you are creating a gift, the draft is kept in the browser you are using. When you finish, the share link carries the card settings needed to open the gift.

Can I make a printable cassette insert?

Yes. Open Custom J cards to make a cassette J card with an album title, dedication and track list.

What should I put in a gift note?

Keep it personal and brief: a shared memory, a reason for choosing the songs or a simple message for the person receiving the gift. Do not include passwords, addresses or other sensitive information.

Where can I find music licence information?

See the Music credits page for the current sources, creators and licences used by the available previews.
