How to Make a Mixtape Online

Learn how to choose a theme, select meaningful songs, arrange them in order, write a personal note, and share a digital mixtape gift.

Make Your Mixtape

What Makes a Good Mixtape?

A good mixtape is more than a list of songs. Give it a clear person, an emotion or a small story. Let the order change a little as it moves, and choose a visual style that fits the occasion. The note should explain why this particular sequence belongs to the person receiving it.

Choose Who It Is For

Start with the person before you start with a song. You can make a mixtape for a partner, a best friend, a birthday recipient, someone who needs comfort, or someone you want to thank. The maker's To and From fields give the gift a clear point of view.

Choose a Theme

Use a Birthday, For a friend, Love & anniversary or A little comfort direction when it matches the moment. Thank-you, Just because and Long distance also work as personal themes, even when you simply choose the songs yourself rather than using a dedicated filter.

Choose the Songs

Start with one song that best represents the gift. Add one to three songs that support its feeling, for a total of up to four. Preview the available openly licensed songs before deciding, and choose for meaning rather than popularity.

Arrange the Songs

With four songs, think of the sequence as a tiny story. The opening creates the first impression. The second song develops the theme. The third can add a memory or emotional change. The closing leaves the final feeling you want the person to keep.

Write the Note

The note can be up to 140 characters, so make one idea count. Explain why you chose the songs, mention a shared memory, write a birthday wish or offer encouragement. A few original prompts:

  • Four songs for the little moments that make me think of you.
  • I made this for the next chapter and every good memory behind us.
  • Press play whenever you need a small reminder that I am here.
  • One song for how it started, two for what we share, and one for what comes next.
  • Happy birthday. I hope this year sounds exactly like you.
  • These are the songs I wanted you to hear today.
  • A quiet collection for a difficult day. No reply needed.
  • Thank you for making ordinary days feel like something worth remembering.

Make It With Custom Mixtape

  1. Choose a cassette background.
  2. Add up to three stickers.
  3. Select one to four songs and adjust their order.
  4. Fill in To, From and your short note.
  5. Finish the card and generate a share link.

Your recipient can open the link in a browser without an account and press play when they are ready.
